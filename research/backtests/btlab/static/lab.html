Glossary: the words used on this page
Base trade
The unchanged rule. Every experiment is measured against it on the same data.
Day change
How far the stock has moved since yesterday's close at the moment of the decision.
RVOL
Relative volume: today's volume so far ÷ the usual volume by this time of day. 3 = three times the normal activity.
VWAP
Volume-weighted average price of the session: the "fair" price most traders benchmark against.
EMA9 / 20 / 200
Exponential moving averages over 9, 20 and 200 bars. Short over long = an uptrend.
MACD
A momentum gauge: the gap between a fast and a slow moving average. Positive = momentum is up.
R
The trade's risk unit: entry minus stop. A 2R target is twice that distance above the entry.
Support / resistance
Price levels where buyers (support, below) or sellers (resistance, above) showed up before. The stop goes under support, the target under resistance.
Real costs
Brokerage, taxes, exchange fees and slippage, itemised. Every ₹ figure on this page is after them.
Median / trade
The middle trade. If the mean is positive and the median is negative, a few big winners carry the result.
Without the 5 best
The mean after removing the five best trades: shows whether the result relies on luck.
Profit factor
Total won ÷ total lost. Above 1 = profitable.
p-value
Roughly, the chance of a difference this large with no real effect. Above 0.1 = indistinguishable from noise.
Trial #
How many experiments were run on this data window. The more you try, the more will look good by chance.

Results here are exploratory. Anything intended to be believed or shipped still needs a pre-registered hypothesis. Manual: research/backtests/README-lab.md.
