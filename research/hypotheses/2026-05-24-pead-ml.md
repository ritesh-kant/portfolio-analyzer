# Hypothesis: PEAD v4 — ML Selection with Reliable Features (No Consensus)

## 1. Hypothesis

A LightGBM classifier trained on 10 pre-registered features — all computable from
NSE filings + OHLCV data, without any third-party consensus data — can identify
the subset of Midcap 150 quarterly earnings events where the stock drifts upward
over the following 5 trading days by ≥ 100 bps net of costs, achieving per-trade
Sharpe ≥ 0.5 on the dev period.

## 2. Why ML Where Rules Failed

Strategy L (rules-based, 2026-05-24) showed that the average event with ≥ 25%
YoY EPS growth has zero edge: mean return −40.6 bps, win rate 44.5%.

The signal is not in the magnitude of YoY growth alone. The critical information
is whether the market has ALREADY priced in the good news by T+1 open, or whether
the news is underappreciated and will continue to drift.

This requires joint conditioning on:

- Earnings quality (how good are the numbers?)
- Market reaction on announcement day (how much has already been priced in?)
- Momentum context (is the stock in an environment where drift is likely?)

Rules-based filters can apply one condition at a time. LightGBM captures
non-linear interactions between features — specifically the interaction between
earnings quality and day-0 market reaction, which is the primary driver of
post-announcement drift.

## 3. Mechanism

The core insight: **a stock that reports strong earnings but barely moves on
announcement day is underappreciated and will drift up over the next 5 days.**

High day-0 reaction → news fully priced in → no drift
Low day-0 reaction + high earnings growth → news not yet discovered → drift potential

The LightGBM model learns this interaction plus supporting features (volume,
momentum, seasonality) to separate drift events from already-priced events.

## 4. Pre-Registered Features (immutable after this commit)

Exactly 10 features, all from NSE filings + OHLCV. No third-party data.

| #   | Feature                  | Source                 | Direction hypothesis         |
| --- | ------------------------ | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1   | `yoy_eps_growth`         | earnings parquet       | Higher → more signal         |
| 2   | `yoy_rev_growth`         | earnings parquet       | Higher → confirms quality    |
| 3   | `net_profit_margin`      | earnings parquet       | Higher → better quality      |
| 4   | `day0_reaction`          | OHLCV prev_close→close | Lower → more residual drift  |
| 5   | `day0_vol_ratio`         | OHLCV volume / 20d avg | Lower → less discovered      |
| 6   | `ema50_dist`             | OHLCV EMA50            | Positive → momentum tailwind |
| 7   | `pre5d_return`           | OHLCV 5-day return     | Moderate positive → momentum |
| 8   | `days_since_last_result` | earnings parquet       | Seasonality proxy            |
| 9   | `quarter_sin`            | fiscal_quarter         | Seasonal cyclicality         |
| 10  | `quarter_cos`            | fiscal_quarter         | Seasonal cyclicality         |

**MAX_FEATURES = 10. Adding features after seeing dev results = process violation.**

## 5. Signal Universe

All Midcap 150 quarterly earnings events where:

- `eps_reported > 0` AND `yoy_eps_prev > 0` (both quarters profitable)
- `yoy_eps_prev` is available (not null)
- All 10 features can be computed

This is a broader universe than Strategy L (no ≥ 25% threshold). The ML model
learns the appropriate EPS growth threshold jointly with other features.

## 6. Model Architecture (pre-registered, immutable)

```
LightGBM binary classifier:
  objective      = binary
  max_depth      = 3          (shallow trees — avoids overfitting on small dataset)
  n_estimators   = 100
  learning_rate  = 0.05
  min_child_samples = 20      (regularization — avoids tiny leaf overfit)
  subsample      = 0.8
  colsample_bytree = 0.8
  random_state   = 42

Probability calibration: isotonic regression on OOF predictions.

Training: purged k-fold (k=5, embargo=10 trading days) on 2015-01-01 → 2023-06-30.
Inference: score all dev events; trade events with P(net return > 100 bps) ≥ 0.5.
```

P-threshold = 0.5 is pre-registered. Do NOT tune on dev results.

## 7. Trading Rule

```python
# For each Midcap 150 quarterly earnings event on date T:
# 1. Compute 10 features as of T close (PIT-correct)
# 2. Score with fitted model: p = model.predict_proba(features)
# 3. If p >= 0.5 AND not election period AND not pledge-flagged:
#    - Entry: T+1 open
#    - Exit: T+5 close
#    - Net return: (exit / entry - 1) - 0.0055
```

**Root cause 1 — Structural threshold failure:**

The pre-registered P threshold of 0.50 was structurally unreachable. The isotonic
calibration collapsed to a plateau: 70% of all dev events mapped to exactly P=0.3899.
Only 8 unique calibrated values exist; max was 0.4677. This is a calibration
distribution shift: training scores and dev scores come from different distributions
because two features are dead (see below).

**Root cause 2 — Critical feature degradation:**

| Feature                  | Dev quality | Issue                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------ | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `net_profit_margin`      | 100% zero   | `net_profit_cr` is NULL for ALL 2023–2024 earnings rows — not a naming issue; the NSE filing pipeline never populated this column for recent quarters |
| `yoy_rev_growth`         | 70% zero    | `yoy_revenue_prev` is NULL for 70% of dev events                                                                                                      |
| `days_since_last_result` | Partial     | Computed within eval window only; prior quarter history not available                                                                                 |

The model was trained on 3 earnings-quality features + 4 OHLCV features but evaluated
on effectively 1 earnings feature (`yoy_eps_growth`) + OHLCV. Training and dev
distributions are fundamentally different.

**Root cause 3 — No underlying signal:**

Even with perfect features, there is no PEAD signal to capture. The full dev universe
(all 753 scored events) returned mean −54 bps net. The PEAD mechanism on Indian midcap
earnings is front-run: institutional/algo traders scan NSE filings at 18:00 IST; by
T+1 open, the good-news trade is fully crowded. This was confirmed by Strategy L
(rules-based PEAD, mean −40.6 bps) and is now reconfirmed with ML selection.

The model's ranking is not statistically significant (Spearman ρ = 0.044, p = 0.225).
ML selection cannot manufacture signal where none exists.

**Full PEAD family (L–M):**

| Strategy      | Type         | Dev mean  | Sharpe | Verdict |
| ------------- | ------------ | --------- | ------ | ------- |
| L (YoY ≥ 25%) | Rules-based  | −40.6 bps | −0.068 | Killed  |
| M (LightGBM)  | ML selection | 0 trades  | N/A    | Killed  |

**Conclusion:** The PEAD signal on NSE Midcap 150 earnings with T+1 open entry
does not exist. Rules-based and ML variants both fail. The mechanism requires
either (a) entry before T+1 open (intraday on filing day), or (b) a different
market where institutional front-running is less complete.

## 11. Decision

**KILLED — 2026-05-25.** Zero dev trades (pre-registered threshold structurally
unreachable), combined with non-significant model ranking and no underlying signal
in the full event universe.

**Full strategy audit (A–M):**

All 13 strategy variants (A–M) across block/bulk deal momentum and PEAD have been
tested and killed. The signal families attempted:

- **Bulk deal momentum (F–I)**: Genuine edge exists (67% win rate, 444 bps mean,
  DSR 0.99) but intrinsic per-trade σ of ~11% prevents Sharpe ≥ 0.5.
- **Bulk deal variants (J, K)**: Stop-loss (J) and 5-day hold (K) both made
  Sharpe worse. The signal is a slow 3–4 week institutional accumulation.
- **PEAD rules-based (L)**: Zero signal — T+1 open fully prices in earnings.
- **PEAD ML (M)**: Data quality failure + no underlying signal to select from.

**Required pivot:** The plan's remaining approved signal families are:

- F&O VRP (volatility risk premium) — requires options data (paid)
- Intraday earnings drift — requires tick data or filing-timestamp data (paid)

Until a paid data source is approved (§plan: post Strategy A paper validation),
the quant research phase is at a natural pause point.
