# Hypothesis (BT59): removing ONLY the EMA9-break exit improves net per trade

## 1. Mechanism

The live rule (`trend_resistance_state`) sells when a completed bar closes under EMA9. On the
2026 live-rule replay that is 18 of 133 exits (13.5%), e.g. AMBER 2023-03-03 sold 2065.50 at
10:30, +0.01%, on an EMA9 break while the 2R target (2125.45) and the checkpoint (2096.85)
were still unreached. Hypothesis: the EMA9 break cuts winners short on ordinary pullbacks.
The other four indicator exits (EMA20 break, MACD fade, resistance reject, volume climax),
the checkpoint stop, the trailing stops, the support-break exit and the 15:15 close stay on.

## 2. Expected effect size

- Direction: long. Same entries, later exits on the trades the EMA9 rule used to close.
- Magnitude: unknown. Prior record: removing the trailing stop = exactly nothing (−₹0.86/trade,
  p=0.56); removing ALL exits looked +₹20.59/trade but median delta ₹0.00 and top 5 trades = 44%
  of the gain. Expect ≈0.

symbol-date + entry time where both traded. The window is SPENT: it can kill the idea, never
